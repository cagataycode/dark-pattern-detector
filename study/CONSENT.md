## What the study is about

This study looks at how people make decisions when shopping online. Some details of the study are only explained afterwards, because knowing them in advance could change how you shop. You'll get a full explanation right after your session.

## What you'll do

1. Shop in a test online shop on this laptop and place one order. It is **not a real shop**: no products are sent and **nothing is charged**.
2. Answer a short questionnaire (about 4 minutes).
3. Get an explanation of the study and ask any questions.

It takes about **15 minutes** in total.

## What is recorded

- **What you do in the test shop**, for example which products you look at, what's in your cart and what you order. It's stored under a number (your session id), **not your name**.
- **Your questionnaire answers**, under the same number.
- **Short notes** by the researcher about what you do or say during the task, also under the number.

**Not recorded:** your name (only on this form), audio, video, screen recordings, or anything you type into the shop's email field. That field is never saved, so you can type anything there.

## How your data is used and kept

- Only the researcher sees the raw data. Results are reported only as totals across all participants, so nobody can be identified.
- The data stays on the researcher's devices and is not shared or published. The results may appear in a university assignment and in a public summary of the project, in aggregated form only.
- This form is kept separately from the data and destroyed after the assignment is graded. The raw data is deleted by [date].

## Your rights

- Taking part is **voluntary**. Saying no or stopping has no consequences of any kind.
- You can **stop at any time** during the session, without giving a reason.
- You can **withdraw your data** until [date] by emailing the researcher with your session id (below). It will then be deleted.
- You can ask questions at any time.

## Risks

None beyond normal online shopping. Some people find it uncomfortable to learn afterwards how a shop tried to influence them. The explanation at the end covers this.
